## Rules Applied

* **Official U.S. Army/DoD medals and ribbons** are kept unchanged.
* **Standard U.S. qualification badges, weapon qualifications, and tabs** are kept unchanged.
* **7CAV-named, game-server, unit citation, and community awards** must receive DDI-owned artwork.
* **No award names, catalog entries, priorities, or sprite sheets were modified.**
* DDI can later choose to rebrand generic awards by moving them to `replace artwork only`.
